Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a paid ChatGPT account? +

No. Curravue is not tied to one AI subscription. The prompt systems are designed to work with leading conversational AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot. Platform limits and capabilities differ by provider and plan.

Do I need to understand prompt engineering? +

No. That is the point. Curravue gives you the detailed instructions and workflow structure. You add the information specific to your lesson, assessment, communication, or planning task.

Can I use Curravue across different grade levels? +

Yes. Many Curravue workflows use grade, course, standards, learner needs, and instructional context as variables. Follow the guidance included with each product and review all AI-generated material before classroom use.

How do school or department licenses work? +

Individual purchases are licensed for the purchaser according to the terms included with the product. Schools, teams, departments, and districts that want broader use should contact Curravue for the appropriate licensing option.
